# About This Website

This website is a personal collection of learning journals, reflections, and explorations, primarily in mathematics, physics, neuroscience and astronomy.

The content is intended to document my learning process rather than serve as a rigorous or authoritative academic resource.

## Accuracy and Limitations

These notes reflect my understanding. Errors, incomplete arguments, informal explanations, or mathematical inaccuracies are inevitable.

Some ideas are exploratory and may not be fully developed or rigorously justified.

## Sources and Attribution

Materials on this website are informed by university courses, textbooks, lecture notes, academic publications, and other educational resources.

I aim to acknowledge major sources and provide references where appropriate. However, given the informal and personal nature of these notes, it may not always be possible to identify or individually attribute every non-original idea, explanation, or influence.

The absence of a citation should not be interpreted as a claim of originality.
